POSITIONING
Open lanes for African money and trade.
Sharet Africa moves payments across East Africa and out to the world, and gives traders the finance to keep goods moving.
TAGLINE OPTIONS
Money moves. Trade grows.
Africa, open for business.
Two ways. No borders.
01
Fast lanes
Payments across borders should feel as quick as paying next door.
02
Clear numbers
Every rate and fee is shown up front, in monospace, with nothing hidden.
03
Trade-ready
Built for importers, exporters and the businesses between them.
VOICE
Direct
Say “Sent. Arrives in [TIME].”
Not “Your transaction has been initiated.”
Confident
Say “Your rate is locked for [DURATION].”
Not “Rates may possibly change.”
Warm
Say “Your supplier in Kampala has been paid.”
Not “Beneficiary credited.”
Pan-African
Say local currency names and city names.
Not “emerging markets”.
