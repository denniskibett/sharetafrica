Logo misuse

07 / GUIDELINES
ALWAYS
Use the supplied SVG files, unchanged, on ink, paper or lime, with clear space of one lane height all round.
Don't put the dark-ground logo on light grounds
Don't stretch or squash
Don't rotate or tilt
Don't recolour the lanes or the dot
Don't add glows, shadows or effects
Don't place on low-contrast colours
Don't rearrange the symbol and wordmark
africa
Don't retype AFRICA in another font or case
