Symbol & type logo

02 / BRAND IDENTITY
SYMBOL
Two lanes, two directions: the lime lane is money going out of Africa, the light lane is money coming in. Stacked, they also read as an “s” and as the ⇄ exchange sign.
Use alone for app icons, avatars, favicons, stamps, and anywhere the name already appears nearby.
TYPE LOGO
“sharet” in Boldonse, converted to outlines, with AFRICA in Geist Mono, right-aligned beneath. Drop AFRICA when space is tight or the region is already clear.
Use the type logo alone in tight horizontal spaces, document headers, and email signatures.
